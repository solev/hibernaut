Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE polished logo exploration moodboard for Hibernaut, a self-hosted developer infrastructure tool. Product: named Linux Firecracker microVMs accessed through ordinary "ssh app.hib"; idle guests snapshot memory AND disk and wake with processes intact; host sleeps to stop compute billing. Planned v1: GitHub/Codex/Copilot connected once; real secrets stay on host, guests hold only useless placeholders, egress proxy swaps them only on approved sites; golden images and forks copy ready live work. Personality calm, capable, economical, precise, curious, quietly playful.

Composition: landscape 4:3 board, exactly FOUR COLUMNS and THREE ROWS, twelve equal spacious tiles. Uniform neutral off-white #F5F3ED background across entire board. Very subtle thin dividers allowed. No header, no footer, no palette chips. Each tile has a generous centered logo in its upper area and a short centered two-line caption in its lower area: concept name in medium-weight sans serif, explanatory line smaller. All captions readable, identically positioned, no overlapping. Preserve order exactly below. Crisp flat vector-style raster artwork, solid colors, clean edges, careful negative space, icon-scale simplicity, coherent premium developer-tool design quality. Each concept must be structurally distinct, not twelve recolorings. Mix 5 standalone symbols, 2 custom wordmarks, and 5 combinations with the exact lowercase word "hibernaut". Make typography deliberate and legible. Spelling h-i-b-e-r-n-a-u-t. Varied restrained 1–2-color palettes on the common off-white board.
